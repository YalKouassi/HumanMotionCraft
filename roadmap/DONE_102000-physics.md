# Physics

- **Status:** done (built by 2026-10-29)
- **Requires:** 101000

## What works
Support, contact, fulcrum (planted feet don't slide), static balance, ballistic flights; applied in
playback and export when the switch is on; `physics.solve` reports problems; overlay with support
area, centre of mass and jump arcs.

they sit up. Not fixed yet; review floor motion with physics off.
