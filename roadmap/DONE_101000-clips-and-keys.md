# Clips and keys

- **Status:** done (built by 2026-10-29)
- **Requires:** 100000

## What works
Keys with 6 interpolations, override/additive layers, links between clips; `key.*`, `keys.retime`
(insert, delete, scale), `keys.copy/paste` (mirror), `key.move_parts` (dope rows), markers,
`feet.plant`, `clip.move` (slide or turn the whole animation).
